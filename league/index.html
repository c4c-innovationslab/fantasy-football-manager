How the plan works

Odds a player is still there for you

Each column is a stretch of picks you own. The number is the chance the player is on the board when that stretch starts. The model walks every opposing pick between now and then, using ADP as the baseline and each team's roster holes to raise or lower the chance they take him. Green is 70 plus, yellow is 40 to 70, red is under 40.
